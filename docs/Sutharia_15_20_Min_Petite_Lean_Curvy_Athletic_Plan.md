# SUTHARIA — 15–20 MIN PETITE LEAN-CURVY ATHLETIC PLAN

**Goal:** Build a petite, lean-curvy athletic physique with rounded glutes, firm proportional legs, a tighter waist, stronger core, better posture, and lightly defined shoulders/arms — with short sessions that fit around a physically active store job.

**Session length:** 15–20 minutes  
**Frequency:** 5 focused training days + 1 light athletic/core day + 1 recovery day  
**Equipment:** Dumbbells, cables, bench, treadmill/bike when available  
**No-equipment rule:** Every loaded exercise has a body-weight or self-resisted alternative.

---

## IMPORTANT TRAINING RULES

- The warm-up is **part of the 15–20 minute session**.
- Use controlled repetitions; do not rush.
- Most working sets should finish with **2–3 good repetitions still possible**.
- Do **not** train to failure.
- Muscle fatigue is okay; **sharp joint pain, swelling, warmth, instability, pelvic pressure, or leakage is not**.
- On a physically demanding store day, reduce the workout to **1–2 sets per exercise** rather than skipping the week entirely.
- If a joint is irritated, use the easier body-weight version and shorten the range of motion.
- Progress by first increasing repetitions, then adding a small amount of weight.

---

# DAILY 2–3 MINUTE WARM-UP RULE

Before every strength session:

1. **Easy march in place** — 30 sec  
2. **Joint circles** for the areas being trained — 20–30 sec  
3. **1–2 movement-specific warm-up drills** — 5–10 slow reps each  
4. First strength set should be slightly easier than the remaining sets.

The specific warm-up for each day is listed below.

# HOW TO PROGRESS

For exercises listed as **2 × 8–12**:

1. Start with a weight she can control comfortably.
2. When she reaches **12 clean reps on both sets** with 2–3 reps still possible:
3. Increase the weight slightly.
4. Return to around 8–9 reps.
5. Build up again.

For body-weight exercises:

1. Add reps.
2. Slow the lowering phase to about 3 seconds.
3. Add a 1-second pause.
4. Increase range of motion.
5. Move to a slightly harder variation.

# BUSY-WORKDAY VERSION — ONLY 8–10 MINUTES

If the store day is extremely tiring, do not force the full workout.

### Lower-body day

- Warm-up — 2 min
- Squat or Split Squat — 2 sets
- Hip Thrust / Glute Bridge — 2 sets
- Hip Abduction — 1 set

### Upper-body day

- Warm-up — 2 min
- Row / Prone W — 2 sets
- Press / Push-Up — 2 sets
- Lateral Raise / Wall Isometric — 1 set

That still keeps the weekly habit alive.

---

# JOINT-SAFETY TRAFFIC LIGHT

## 🟢 Green

- Pain 0–2/10
- No unusual warmth or swelling
- Normal movement
- Symptoms return to baseline by next morning

**Action:** Train normally.

## 🟡 Yellow

- Mild stiffness or pain around 3–4/10
- Mild tenderness
- More fatigue than usual

**Action:**

- Reduce load/reps/sets by 25–50%
- Use body-weight version
- Reduce range of motion
- Keep movement controlled

## 🔴 Red

- Visible swelling
- Redness or increased warmth
- Significant pain
- Joint instability
- Major loss of function
- Symptoms persist or worsen

**Action:** Avoid loading that joint and seek appropriate medical evaluation when significant or recurrent.

# SIMPLE PHYSIQUE PRIORITY

For the petite lean-curvy athletic goal:

1. **Glutes**
2. **Proportional legs**
3. **Upper back and posture**
4. **Shoulders**
5. **Core/waist control**
6. **Arms**
7. **Cardiovascular fitness and mobility**

The goal is **not maximum muscle size**. The goal is consistent strength, shape, posture, joint health, and sustainable fat loss/body recomposition.

---

# NUTRITION REMINDER

Short workouts can still produce results when nutrition is consistent.

A practical starting framework from the longer plan:

- Protein: approximately **100–115 g/day**
- Avoid aggressive crash dieting
- Include protein in every major meal
- Include vegetables, fruit, fibre-rich carbohydrates, and measured healthy fats
- Hydrate consistently, especially during long store shifts
- Adjust calories using average body weight, waist measurement, energy, hunger, and training performance rather than day-to-day scale changes

---

# FINAL RULE

> **Consistency beats duration.**

A focused **15–20 minute workout performed regularly** is far more useful than a 60-minute program that does not fit her work and family schedule.

On demanding workdays, reduce the dose.  
On good-energy days, train with progressive resistance.  
Over months, the combination of **glute-focused strength + balanced upper-body work + core/posture + protein + sensible nutrition** is the strategy for the desired petite lean-curvy athletic physique.
